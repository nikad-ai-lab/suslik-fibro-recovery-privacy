Suslik Fibro Recovery Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers the independently developed iOS preview, also known as “Суслик в тумане”, including its optional WHOOP connection. The developer and privacy contact can be reached at nikad.ai.lab@gmail.com.

Your local diary

Wellbeing ratings, symptoms, notes, events, drafts, dates, time zones, preferences and quest progress are stored locally on your iPhone using SwiftData. Your diary is not uploaded to our server, WHOOP or AI services. CloudKit synchronization is disabled. Device or computer backups may include your diary. Optional reminders run locally with your permission.

Optional WHOOP connection

Connecting is voluntary and requires your authorization at WHOOP. The requested permissions are read:recovery, read:sleep and read:cycles, plus offline to refresh access without repeated sign-in. We do not request profile, email, body measurement or workout permissions.

When you manually synchronize, a separate backend on Hostinger retrieves WHOOP recovery, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep and daily Strain for the last seven days. These readings travel over HTTPS through the backend to your iPhone. Provider responses are processed in memory and are not saved to the backend database. The app displays readings in memory, without adding them to the diary or export or using them in FRS, Radar Lite or check-in calculations.

Credentials and retention

WHOOP passwords are entered only at WHOOP. Our Client Secret stays on the server. WHOOP access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest on the backend with AES-256-GCM; the encryption key is stored separately. The backend stores a hashed app-session identifier and the time of the last successful synchronization. The app keeps its own backend-session credential and synchronization timestamp in device-only Keychain, not WHOOP tokens.

Authorization attempts expire after ten minutes. Unclaimed grants are revoked during cleanup. Connection credentials remain until you disconnect, authorization expires or is revoked, or cleanup follows 90 days without synchronization. Failed revocations are retried: encrypted credentials are retained solely to complete revocation, while synchronization is blocked.

Disconnecting and deleting

Use “Отключить WHOOP” to revoke access and remove stored tokens. After confirmed revocation the server deletes credentials and connection metadata, and the app clears its session key, displayed readings and timestamp. If WHOOP is unavailable, the app shows that revocation is pending. You can also revoke the integration through WHOOP. Server snapshots, if enabled by the hosting provider, may retain encrypted copies under the provider's backup retention; revoked tokens cannot authorize further access.

You can delete local app data in Settings. If WHOOP is connected, the updated app first requests disconnection. User-selected JSON and CSV exports and device backups must be deleted separately from their storage locations.

Hosting and other processing

Hostinger hosts the backend and may process infrastructure metadata. WHOOP processes authorization and supplies authorized account data under its own privacy terms. Hosting and provider processing may occur outside your country. The application has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics or AI data sharing. Backend request logging is disabled; authorization codes, credentials, tokens and health readings are not written to application logs.

This static policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. It contains no analytics, forms or third-party scripts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Hostinger Privacy Policy.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact nikad.ai.lab@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, access tokens or sensitive health records by email.
