Suslik Fibro Recovery Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Daily Sync

This policy covers the independently developed iOS preview, also known as “Суслик в тумане”, including its optional WHOOP connection. Contact: nikad.ai.lab@gmail.com.

Your local diary and history

Wellbeing ratings, symptoms, notes, events, drafts, dates, time zones, preferences and quest progress are stored locally on your iPhone using SwiftData. Authorized WHOOP daily summaries are also stored locally so they remain available offline and after restarting the app. CloudKit synchronization is disabled. Device or computer backups may include this information. Optional reminders run locally with your permission. Your diary and FRS ratings are not uploaded to our backend or WHOOP.

Optional WHOOP connection and synchronization

Connecting is voluntary and requires authorization at WHOOP. The requested permissions are read:recovery, read:sleep and read:cycles, plus offline for token refresh. We do not request profile, email, body measurement or workout permissions.

A separate backend on Hostinger retrieves authorized Recovery, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep, daily Strain and steps where available. Readings travel over HTTPS through the backend to your iPhone. Provider responses are processed in memory, without storing health readings in the backend database.

While connected, opening or returning to the app can update the last seven days when the previous successful update is at least one hour old or belongs to an earlier local day. You can also update manually. There is no scheduled server or push-based background synchronization. A separate optional import of 30 days requires explicit confirmation inside the app and never starts automatically. New requests can revise previously imported days. Local history remains until you delete it.

WHOOP Recovery and FRS are shown as separate measures. Their difference, Fibro Gap, is displayed only for comparable dates and time zones. WHOOP readings and Fibro Gap do not change FRS, Radar Lite or check-in calculations and are not a validated crash prediction.

Credentials and limited server retention

WHOOP passwords are entered only at WHOOP. Our Client Secret stays on the server. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM using a separately stored key. Refresh tokens are rotated when WHOOP supplies replacements. The backend keeps a hashed app-session identifier, connection state, creation time, last successful synchronization time and, after Disconnect, a disconnection time. The iPhone keeps its own backend-session credential in device-only Keychain, not WHOOP tokens; synchronization time is also stored with local history.

Authorization attempts expire after ten minutes and are cleaned up within the next maintenance cycle, with up to five additional minutes for an in-flight exchange. Encrypted unclaimed tokens are then deleted locally without contacting WHOOP to revoke a grant. Only one active attempt or connection is supported for this private preview. Inactive connection credentials are deleted after 90 days without a successful synchronization. After Disconnect they are kept encrypted for at most seven days to allow Resume or an explicit Revoke, then deleted. Expired credentials cannot continue synchronization through the app. Cleanup does not automatically revoke WHOOP permission. After local expiry or an interrupted connection, manage any remaining permission in WHOOP settings.

Disconnect, Revoke and deletion

Disconnect stops synchronization and keeps your local history and WHOOP permission. Resume is available during the seven-day retention period. “Revoke WHOOP access” is a separate confirmation: after WHOOP confirms revocation the server removes tokens and connection metadata, and the app removes its session credential. If revocation fails, synchronization stays blocked and the app asks you to retry; it never reports an unconfirmed revocation as completed. Retained credentials remain subject to the limits above. You can revoke the integration directly in WHOOP at any time.

“Delete imported data” deletes only local WHOOP history and its local synchronization timestamp. Check-ins and permission remain. New synchronization can download readings again, so use Disconnect first if you want updates to stop. Revocation does not erase your local history; delete it separately. Settings also offers deletion of all local app data. User-created exports and device backups must be deleted separately.

Exports and sharing

WHOOP export is disabled by default. Only if you enable “Include WHOOP in export” will an export include WHOOP summaries in JSON and a separate CSV with dates and units. You choose where to save or share the files. The app does not automatically share health or diary data with Hermes, n8n, LLMs, AI services, advertising systems or third-party analytics.

Hosting and logs

Hostinger hosts the backend and may process infrastructure metadata. WHOOP processes authorization and supplies authorized account data under its own terms. Processing may occur outside your country. Backend request logging is disabled; codes, credentials, tokens and health readings are not written to application logs. Private operational backups or hosting snapshots may retain encrypted tokens under their backup retention; copies of a revoked token cannot authorize further access.

This policy is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. This page contains no analytics, forms or third-party scripts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Hostinger Privacy Policy.
