Suslik Fibro Recovery Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers the current local-only iOS preview of Suslik Fibro Recovery, also known as “Суслик в тумане”.

Information processed by the app

The app stores the wellbeing ratings, optional symptoms, notes, events and drafts you enter, together with dates, time zones, preferences and quest progress. It uses this information on your device to display your diary, charts, reminders and experimental wellbeing indicators.

Storage and sharing

Your diary is stored locally on your iPhone. The current app has no account registration, developer-operated data server, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics. It does not send your diary to the developer, AI services or wearable providers. CloudKit synchronization is disabled. Depending on your device settings, Apple or computer backups may include app data.

If you choose to export JSON or CSV files, iOS shares them only with the destination you select. Those copies are then subject to that destination's privacy practices.

Reminders and wearable data

Optional reminders are scheduled locally with your permission. The current preview does not connect to WHOOP or other wearable accounts. Any displayed wearable demo values are fictional and are not saved in your diary or exported. This policy will be updated before a connected WHOOP feature is enabled.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. You can delete all local app data in Settings and disable reminders. Deleting local data does not remove copies you exported or existing device backups; manage these separately with their storage provider.

This policy website

When this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. The page itself uses no analytics, forms or third-party scripts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
